## what it does

a single public room. no dms, no threads, no channels. everyone who opens the
page sees the same conversation.

real events on the site appear in the room as system lines, so the room is partly
a log of what the site is doing.

## how to use

1. open the page to read; no account is needed for that
2. use the sign-in link under the room — it drops you in the users shell, which
   is where signing in happens
3. post to the room — everyone reading sees it
4. click a handle on any message to open that person's intro as a card above the
   room, and close to dismiss it
