## what it does

a page of buttons being buttons. it is a showcase, not a control panel — nothing
here changes anything anywhere else on the site.

two buttons in the intro have pupils that track your cursor. one opens
deflock.org in a new tab; the round one is called "play dead" and does exactly
that, which is the joke.

below them is a red button labelled "do not press". pressing it scrambles the
text of the whole page into symbols until you press it again. nothing is saved
and nothing breaks; the page puts itself back.

the panel on the right is the straight part: the four variants of the shared
button — primary, secondary, tertiary, and glass — at small, medium, and large.
the rainbow line behind the glass row is a page backdrop, not part of the
component. it is there so the blur has something to blur.

## how to use

1. move the cursor around the intro and watch the pupils follow it
2. press "do not press"
3. press it again to put the page back
4. scan the panel on the right for the variants and sizes the rest of the site is
   built from
