## what it does

the sign-in and sign-up form. accounts are what let you post in the room and keep
an intro of your own.

signed-out visitors who try the dashboard land here.

## how to use

1. choose sign in or create an account
2. signing up asks for a name, an email, and a password; signing in only needs
   the username and password
3. usernames are 2–32 letters, numbers, underscores, or hyphens
4. passwords need at least 6 characters and must fit in 72 bytes — accented
   characters count for more than one
5. after signing in you land on the dashboard
6. to sign out, use `logout` in the users shell
