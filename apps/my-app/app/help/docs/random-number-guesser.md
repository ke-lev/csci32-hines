## what it does

pick a range, then guess. every answer narrows the valid interval, and the game
shows you the midpoint of what is left — which is the mathematically best next
guess, if you want it.

## how to use

1. set the low and high bounds; both are inclusive
2. enter a guess and read the higher-or-lower response
3. take the recommended midpoint to finish in the fewest tries, or ignore it
4. after the round, replay the same range or reconfigure from scratch
