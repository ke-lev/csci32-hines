## what it does

an auto-running life field. it never waits for you to press start — the field is
always evolving, and what you add gets folded into whatever is already happening.

planting drops a stable 2-by-2 colony, which survives on its own until something
else reaches it.

## how to use

1. click an empty cell to plant a colony, or click a living one to erase
2. press and drag to keep painting or erasing without releasing
3. use the keyboard to plant at the focused cell if you would rather not drag
4. plant near an existing pattern to watch the two interfere
