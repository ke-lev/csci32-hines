## and then

hastily added some new pages just for the hell of it, such as...

`/timeline/` is a slick little timeline thang where this devlog will live

- the timeline is fixed in the semester beginning/end range
- new posts that follow the `M-D.md` naming scheme get auto added at the appropriate spot in that range 😎 defaults to post closest to visitor's request date
- clicking a point swaps the post inside the card and switches the header with the title/subtitle without reloading the entire page

`/buttons/` is a page that, despite the name, does not contain any buttons... yet
